Curriculum Framework

BS Cyber Security Program (4 Years / 8 Semesters)

The Bachelor of Science in Cyber Security program is designed to align with its mission, Program Educational Objectives (PEOs), and Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs). Each course includes 1 hour of theory and 3 hours of lab work per week, providing students with both foundational knowledge and practical skills for success in the cybersecurity field.
