About Our Department

Excellence in Cybersecurity Education

The Cyber Security Department at QUEST Nawabshah was established in 2022 under the Faculty of Science as a stream program of the Department of Information Technology. We offer a 4-year BS Cyber Security program designed to prepare students for the growing cybersecurity industry. Our department is equipped with 6 qualified PhD faculty members, advanced research labs, and an industry-focused curriculum that prepares students for real-world cybersecurity challenges.
