About Cyber Security Department

Building knowledge, developing skills and preparing students for the digital world.

Empowering the Next Generation of Cybersecurity Professionals

The Cyber Security Department at Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science & Technology, Nawabshah, focuses on academic learning, practical knowledge and research in the field of information and digital security.

Our academic environment encourages students to understand modern cybersecurity challenges, develop problem-solving abilities and explore emerging technologies.

  • Practical and theoretical learning.
  • Cybersecurity awareness and research.
  • Technical skills and innovation.
  • Professional development opportunities.
