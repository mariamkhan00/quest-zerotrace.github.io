About Our Laboratories

The laboratories of the Cyber Security Department support practical activities, technical demonstrations and hands-on exploration of cybersecurity concepts. They provide an environment for understanding how computer systems, networks and digital information can be protected.

Laboratory Rules & Guidelines

General guidelines for maintaining a safe, responsible and productive laboratory environment.

Follow instructions provided by the laboratory supervisor.
Use computers and equipment responsibly.
Do not access systems or networks without authorization.
Keep laboratory equipment and workstations organized.
Follow cybersecurity ethics and data privacy practices.
Report technical problems to the laboratory supervisor.
