Network Security Lab

Supervisor: Dr. Adnan Ahmed

The Network Security Lab is the primary practical learning center of the Department of Cyber Security at QUEST Nawabshah. The lab is a state-of-the-art facility equipped with high-performance workstations, Cisco routers and switches, firewalls, and the latest network security tools.

The lab is designed to bridge the gap between theory and practice. It provides students with hands-on training in network design and configuration, firewall implementation, intrusion detection, vulnerability assessment, and penetration testing. This practical exposure prepares students to meet the challenges of the modern cybersecurity industry.
