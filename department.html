About Cyber Security Department

Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science & Technology, Nawabshah

University Vision

To become a leading educational and research institute that contributes effectively towards building indigenous technological capabilities, ensuring a secure and sustainable digital future for the socio-economic development of Pakistan.

University Mission

To equip our undergraduate and postgraduate students with advanced knowledge through collaborative opportunities, fostering linkages with academia, industry, and government to meet modern technological challenges.

Program Mission

Our mission is to educate and prepare cybersecurity professionals with deep technical expertise, enabling them to address the challenging issues of the digital world. We aim to impart knowledge and skills that safeguard national cyber infrastructure, ensuring a secure environment for society.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

The objectives of the BS Cyber Security degree program are:

Research & Academia

To train students with the technical knowledge and skills required to protect computer networks and systems, enabling them to solve complex security problems.

Engineering Skills

To produce graduates who can design, plan, and implement robust security mechanisms to neutralize cyber threats while collaborating efficiently in teams.

Ethics & Society

To prepare students capable of monitoring, analyzing, and predicting cyber threats while understanding the broader societal and ethical implications of their work.

Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)

Following are the Graduate Attributes defined for the BS Cyber Security Program:

1
Academic Education

To prepare graduates as computing professionals.

2
Knowledge for Solving Problems

Apply knowledge of computing fundamentals and domain knowledge to conceptualize computing models.

3
Problem Analysis

Identify, formulate, and solve complex computing problems using fundamental principles.

4
Design / Development of Solutions

Design and evaluate solutions for complex computing problems with consideration for public health and safety.

5
Modern Tool Usage

Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques and modern computing tools to complex activities.

6
Individual and Team Work

Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams.

7
Communication

Communicate effectively with the computing community and society at large.

8
Computing Professionalism

Understand and assess societal, legal, and cultural issues within local and global contexts.

9
Ethics

Understand and commit to professional ethics, responsibilities, and norms of computing practice.

10
Life-long Learning

Recognize the need, and have the ability, to engage in independent learning for continual development.
