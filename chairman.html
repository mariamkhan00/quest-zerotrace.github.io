Leadership & Vision

Messages from the Dean and Chairman

The Department of Cyber Security at QUEST is committed to providing quality education and research opportunities in modern digital defense technologies. We aim to equip our students with the knowledge, technical skills, and professional ethics required to excel in academia, industry, and research. We encourage our students to pursue innovation and critical thinking as they contribute to the advancement of cybersecurity and society.

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Ibrahim Channa

Dean, Faculty of Science

Welcome to the Cyber Security Department. In today's digital age, cybersecurity is not just a necessity but a foundation for national security and economic stability. Our department is dedicated to producing highly skilled professionals who can protect Pakistan's digital infrastructure from modern cyber threats. We provide a state-of-the-art learning environment, expert faculty, and practical training to ensure our graduates are industry-ready. I invite young minds to join us in this noble mission of building a safer digital future.

Dr. Adnan Ahmed

Chairman, Cyber Security Department
