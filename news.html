BSCYS QUIZ & CTF Hackathon

October 15, 2026 | Events

The Cyber Security Department at Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science & Technology (QUEST), Nawabshah, successfully organized a highly competitive Capture The Flag (CTF) Hackathon and Quiz. The event brought together talented students from various batches to test their practical cybersecurity skills.

Students participated with great enthusiasm, solving complex challenges related to cryptography, web exploitation, reverse engineering, and digital forensics. The primary objective of this event was to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world industry requirements.

The worthy Chairman of the Department, along with faculty members, appreciated the participants' efforts. Winners were awarded certificates and cash prizes to encourage their journey in the field of cybersecurity. Such initiatives reflect the department's commitment to producing world-class cyber defenders for Pakistan.
