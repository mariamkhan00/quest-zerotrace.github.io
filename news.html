HECK READY 2026 - MINI CTF

October 21, 2026 | Competition

The Cyber Security Department at QUEST successfully organized the HECK READY 2026 - Mini Capture The Flag (CTF) competition on October 21, 2026. The event brought together talented students who are passionate about ethical hacking and cybersecurity.

This Mini CTF was designed to test the practical skills of students in various domains, including Web Exploitation, Cryptography, Reverse Engineering, and Digital Forensics. Participants faced real-world challenges in a controlled environment, which helped bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and industry practices.

The competition saw overwhelming participation and enthusiasm from students across different batches. The worthy Chairman, along with faculty members, appreciated the participants for their problem-solving skills and teamwork. Such initiatives are crucial for building the next generation of cybersecurity professionals in Pakistan.
